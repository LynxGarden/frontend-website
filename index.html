Step 01

Set up on the reformer

Feet on the bar, shoulders against the blocks. The instructor sets your spring tension before you do anything else.

POV: ribcage rising, hands on stomach
Step 02

Find the breath

Four counts in, six out. Everything after this depends on it, which is why nobody rushes this part.

POV: hands on the straps, carriage moving
Step 03

Load the springs

Footwork, then long stretch. The carriage moves slowly on purpose — slow is where the control lives.

POV: instructor correcting your position
Step 04

Deliberate core work

Six people in the room, so the correction comes to you by name. Ten good repetitions beat thirty rushed ones.

POV: walking down the corridor to physio
Step 05

Once a week: physiotherapy

Something in the left hip is not tracking. The physio room is at the end of the corridor and Thursday is free.

Questions

Before you come in.

Do I need a referral to book physiotherapy?

No. You can book an assessment directly, and you don't have to be a member. If something turns up, we'll tell you what training around it looks like.

I've never used a reformer. Is that a problem?

Every first class is an intro class. Six people per session means the instructor gets to you within the first minute.

Can I use only one of the three?

Yes. The Floor membership is gym only, and physiotherapy can be booked as a single session. Most people end up using two.

What should I bring on the first visit?

Indoor shoes, a towel and grip socks for pilates — we sell those at the desk if you forget.
